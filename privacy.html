Bounce Empire Stats - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Bounce Empire Stats is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @bounceempire.tv to follow the statistics of the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

After the account owner logs in with TikTok and gives consent, the tool reads, through the TikTok API: the basic profile of that account (user.info.basic) and the list of its own videos with their public counters - views, likes, comments and shares (video.list).

Where the data goes

The data is saved only on the owner's own computer, in a local file used by the tool. It is not sent to any server, not shared with or sold to anyone, and not used for advertising.

How to remove access

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from the tool itself or from the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services). Disconnecting stops any further access; the local file can be deleted at any time.

Contact

For questions, contact the account @bounceempire.tv on TikTok.
